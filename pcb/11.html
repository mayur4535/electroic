Diagrams & Theory

A microstrip trace is placed on the top surface of the PCB above a reference plane. Required width increases with current and decreases with thicker copper or higher allowed temperature rise.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Step 1: Convert current, thickness and length using selected unit multipliers.
Step 2: Required cross section is calculated from the original microstrip polynomial using selected temperature rise and current.
Step 3: Trace width = required cross section / copper thickness / selected output width unit.
Step 4: Trace temperature = ambient temperature + selected temperature rise.
Step 5: Resistance = abs(17e-7 × length_cm / cross_section_cm² × (1 + 0.0039 × (trace_temperature - 25))).
Step 6: Voltage drop = resistance × current.
Step 7: Power dissipation = voltage drop × current.
