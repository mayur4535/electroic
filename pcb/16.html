Diagrams & Theory

A wire microstrip uses a round conductor above a dielectric substrate and ground plane. Its impedance changes with wire diameter, substrate height and dielectric constant.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Effective Er = (Er + 1) / 2 + (Er - 1) / 2 × correction factor.
A = (2 × H + D) / D.
Z0 = 377 / (2 × π × sqrt(effective Er)) × log(A + sqrt(A² - 1)).
D = wire diameter, H = substrate height, Er = dielectric constant.
