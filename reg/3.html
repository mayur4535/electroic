Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Series: Ltotal = L1 + L2 + L3 + ...
Parallel: Ltotal = 1 / (1/L1 + 1/L2 + 1/L3 + ...)

Cascading string configurations accumulate magnetic coil lengths directly to heighten tracking Henry limits, while paired parallel shunts divide total current bounds to decrease overall system lumped properties.
