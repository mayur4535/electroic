Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Single layer: L(µH) = (r² × N² × μr) / (9r + 10l)
Multi layer: L(µH) = (0.8 × r² × N² × μr) / (6r + 9l + 10d)
Flat mode: delta = n × (w + s) - s; Din = Dout - 2 × delta; Davg = Dout - delta; p = (Dout - Davg) / Davg.
Flat mode uses Modified Wheeler, Current Sheet and Monomial Fit constants for the selected shape only.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is coil inductance?

Coil inductance is the ability of a winding to store magnetic energy when current flows through it.

What does μr mean?

μr is relative permeability. Use μr = 1 for air-core coils and higher values for magnetic materials.

Why are there three flat spiral formulas?

Modified Wheeler, Current Sheet and Monomial Fit are different published approximation methods for planar spiral inductors.

What is calculated inner diameter?

It is the inner opening left after subtracting turns, trace width and spacing from outer diameter.

What is fill factor p?

It describes how tightly the spiral is packed according to the average diameter relation used in the formula.

Are these results exact?

No. They are engineering estimates. Real measured inductance may vary due to construction and material effects.

Can I use this for PCB inductors?

Yes, use Flat Planar Spiral Coil mode and select the matching shape.
