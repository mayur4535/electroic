Formulas

K = 10^(Attenuation / 20)

R1 > Z0 = Z0 × Ratio

Diagrams & Theory

A reflection attenuator uses resistor values based on attenuation and characteristic impedance.

This calculator gives two possible resistor values: one greater than Z0 and one lower than Z0.

Example

Input Example

Attenuation: 10 dB
Impedance: 50 Ω

Output

The calculator converts attenuation to voltage ratio and calculates both reflection attenuator resistor values.
