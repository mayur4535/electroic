Diagram & Theory
A capacitor creates opposition to AC current called capacitive reactance. Higher frequency or higher capacitance reduces reactance. Admittance is the inverse of reactance.
Formulas
Xc = 1000 / (2 × π × fGHz × CpF)
C = 1000 / (2 × π × fGHz × Xc)
Y = 1 / Xc
Example
If capacitance = 10 pF and frequency = 1 GHz:
Reactance = 15.92 Ω
Admittance = 0.0628 S
