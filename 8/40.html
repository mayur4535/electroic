T-Pad Attenuator Diagram

Formulas

K = 10^(Attenuation / 20)
R1 = Z0 × ((K - 1) / (K + 1))
R2 = 2 × Z0 × (K / (K² - 1))

Diagrams & Theory

A T-pad attenuator uses two equal series resistors and one shunt resistor to reduce signal level while maintaining impedance matching.
