Formulas Used

Free Space Loss = C + 20 × log10(Distance) + 20 × log10(Frequency)
C = 36.6 for miles, C = 32.5 for km
Effective Radiated Power = Tx Power - Tx Cable Loss + Tx Antenna Gain
Rx Signal Strength = ERP - Free Space Loss + Rx Antenna Gain - Rx Cable Loss
Fade Margin = Rx Signal Strength - Rx Sensitivity

Diagrams & Theory

This RF link budget calculator estimates whether a wireless link has enough received signal level. Higher transmit power, higher antenna gain, lower cable loss, and shorter distance improve the final link margin.

Fade Margin

Fade margin is the safety difference between receiver signal strength and receiver sensitivity. A larger fade margin gives better tolerance against rain, obstruction, cable loss, antenna alignment error, and RF fading.

Free Space Loss

Free space loss increases when distance or frequency increases. This calculator supports miles and kilometers while using the same constants from the original calculator logic.

Example

For 2440 MHz, 23 dBm transmit power, 9 dB transmit antenna gain, 24 dB receive antenna gain, 0.9 dB cable loss on both sides, 10 miles distance, and -90 dBm receiver sensitivity, click Calculate to find fade margin, free space loss, and receiver signal strength.
