This original SVG diagram shows transmitter power, antenna gain, free-space loss, receiver gain, and received signal strength.

Formulas

Free Space Loss = C + 20 × log10(Distance) + 20 × log10(Frequency)
C = 36.6 for miles, C = 32.5 for km
ERP = Tx Power - Tx Cable Loss + Tx Antenna Gain
Rx Signal Strength = ERP - Free Space Loss + Rx Antenna Gain - Rx Cable Loss
Fade Margin = Rx Signal Strength - Rx Sensitivity

Diagrams & Theory

RF link budget estimates whether a wireless link has enough received signal level. Higher transmit power, antenna gain, and lower cable loss improve the final margin.

Free space loss increases with distance and frequency. A positive fade margin gives tolerance against fading, rain, obstruction, alignment error, and other real-world losses.
