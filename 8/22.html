Diagram & Theory
Coax impedance depends on conductor diameter, shield inner diameter and dielectric constant.
Formulas
Zo = 60 / √εr × ln(d2 / d1)
Delay = 84.72 × 10⁻³ × √εr
Inductance = 5.08 × ln(d2 / d1)
Capacitance = 1.41 / ln(d2 / d1)
Example
d1 = 20 mil, d2 = 67 mil, εr = 2.25
Impedance ≈ 48.3 Ω
Delay ≈ 0.127 ns/in
Inductance ≈ 6.14 nH/in
Capacitance ≈ 1.17 pF/in
