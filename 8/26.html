Formulas

Pr = Pt × Gt × Gr × (λ / 4πd)²
λ = c / f
Gt linear = 10Gt(dBi) / 10
Gr linear = 10Gr(dBi) / 10
d = Distance in meters, f = Frequency in Hz

Diagrams & Theory

Friis transmission equation estimates ideal received power in free space. Antenna gains entered in dBi are converted to linear gain before calculation.

Higher frequency and longer distance reduce received power. Higher transmitter and receiver gain increase received power.

Example

Input Example

Transmitter Power: 100 mW
Transmitter Gain: 2 dBi
Frequency: 2.4 GHz
Distance: 10 m
Receiver Gain: 2 dBi

How to Use

Enter transmitter power, antenna gains in dBi, frequency, and distance. The calculator returns received power in the selected power unit.
