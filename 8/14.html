Input Parameters Specification

Velocity Factor (U) The fraction parameter representing transmission propagation speed inside the cable medium relative to light velocity constants in pure vacuum.
Round Trip Time (Td) The full temporal timeframe step gap (seconds) required for the incident voltage step edge to travel down the path length and reflect back to the source.
