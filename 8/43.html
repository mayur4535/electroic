Rectangular Waveguide Structure

A rectangular waveguide is a hollow metallic pipe used to guide high-frequency radio waves, specifically microwaves. The dominant mode of propagation is typically the TE10 mode, which depends exclusively on the inner broad wall dimension.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

The system calculates parameters for the fundamental lowest-order transverse electric mode (TE10) where height requirements are non-restrictive to cutoff thresholds:

Cutoff Frequency (fc):
fc = c / (2 * a)

Where 'c' is the speed of light (approximately 2.99792458 x 10^8 m/s) and 'a' is the internal broad wall width dimension converted to meters.
Recommended Operating Range:
Lower Bound = 1.25 * fc
Upper Bound = 1.89 * fc
