Skin Depth Diagram

Formulas

δ = √(ρ / (π × f × μr × μ0))
μ0 = 4π × 10⁻⁷ H/m
Frequency: GHz is converted to MHz, then MHz to Hz
Resistivity: µΩ·cm is converted using ρ × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m

Diagrams & Theory

Skin depth is the distance from the conductor surface where AC current density falls significantly.

As frequency increases, current flows closer to the surface, so skin depth becomes smaller.

Example

Input Example

Material: Copper
Resistivity: 1.678 µΩ·cm
μr: 1
Frequency: 10 GHz

Output
