Formulas & Mathematical Logic

RMS Phase Jitter Calculation (Radians Notation):
RMS Phase Jitter (Rad) = sqrt( 2 * 10^(PN / 10) )
RMS Time-Domain Jitter Equation (Micro-Seconds uS Matrix):
RMS Phase Jitter (uS) = [ PhaseJitter / (2 * pi * f) ] * 10^6
