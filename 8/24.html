Diagrams & Theory

EIRP means the effective radiated power in the strongest antenna direction after cable loss and antenna gain are included.
Formulas

Original formula preserved:

If input is mW: Power(dBm) = 10 × log10(mW)
If input is W: Power(dBm) = 10 × log10(W × 1000)
If input is dBW: Power(dBm) = dBW + 30

EIRP = Power(dBm) − Cable Loss(dB) + Antenna Gain(dBi)
Example

Power = 30 dBm, Cable Loss = 2 dB, Antenna Gain = 10 dBi
EIRP = 30 − 2 + 10 = 38 dBm
