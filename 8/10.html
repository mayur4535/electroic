Input Parameters Specification

Effective Heights (Ht / Hr) The physical-to-electromagnetic effective aperture center heights of both the radiating transmitting wire and intercept receiving station.
Antenna Current (I) The excitation current amplitude driven inside the primary element radiator circuit grid, natively computed in Amperes (A).
Distance (d) The exact baseline spatial path length separation segment between the transmitter structure and measurement verification target plane.
Operating Frequency (f) The periodic wave cycle velocity factor driven inside the source network, defining propagation characteristics across mediums.

Antenna Wave Radiation and Field Strength Layout

The system maps electromagnetic induction paths from a source driver amplifier out into free space via logging element arrays inside isolated validation structures.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Electromagnetic Wave Transmission Wavelength (λ Formula):
λ = 3 × 10⁸ / f
RF Field Strength Calculation Formulation:
RF Field Strength = (120 × π × Ht × Hr × I) / (λ × d)

Where 120 × π (approximately 377 Ohms) sets the uniform wave characteristic impedance of free space, scaling the induction ratio across geometric paths linearly.
