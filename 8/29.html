Noise Figure and Noise Temperature Diagram

Formulas

Noise Temperature = Reference Temperature × (10^(Noise Figure / 10) - 1)
Noise Figure = 10 × log10((Noise Temperature / Reference Temperature) + 1)

Diagrams & Theory

Noise figure describes how much extra noise an RF device adds to a signal path. Lower noise figure means better receiver sensitivity and cleaner signal performance.

Noise temperature is another way to express the same noise contribution, commonly used in RF receiver, antenna, and microwave system calculations.

Input Example

Reference Temperature: 290 K
