Input Parameters Specification

Third Order Intercept (TOI) The theoretical point where the power of third-order intermodulation products equals the fundamental output power, measured in dBm.
Input Power Level (PIN) The baseline operational RF signal excitation amplitude level injected at the input port terminal boundary of the device under test.
Gain of the DUT (G) The characteristic absolute linear power amplification transmission gain coefficient of the active component framework, measured in dB.
