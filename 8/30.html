Cascaded Noise Figure Diagram

Formulas

Ftotal = F1 + (F2 - 1)/G1 + (F3 - 1)/(G1 × G2) + ...
NFtotal(dB) = 10 × log10(Ftotal)
Total Gain(dB) = G1(dB) + G2(dB) + G3(dB) + ...
Noise Temperature = 290 × (10^(NFtotal/10) - 1)

Diagrams & Theory

Cascaded noise figure is used for RF receiver chains with multiple amplifiers, filters, mixers, or other gain stages.

The first stage is very important because its gain reduces the noise contribution of later stages. This calculator uses the standard Friis cascaded noise figure relation.

Stage 2: NF 3 dB, Gain 10 dB

How to Use
