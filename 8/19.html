Formulas

w = 2π × Cutoff / 1000
wbw = 2π × Passband / 1000
Series L = g × Z / wbw
Series C = 1000 × wbw / (g × Z × w²)
Shunt L = wbw × Z / (g × w²)
Shunt C = 1000 × g / (wbw × Z)

Diagrams & Theory
