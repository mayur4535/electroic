Formulas

w = 2π × Cutoff Frequency / 1000
wbw = 2π × Passband / 1000
Series L = g × Z / wbw
Series C = 1000 × wbw / (g × Z × w²)
Shunt L = wbw × Z / (g × w²)
Shunt C = 1000 × g / (wbw × Z)

Diagrams & Theory

An LC bandpass filter passes signals around the selected frequency band and attenuates frequencies below and above that band. The calculator gives two possible ladder arrangements: Type-1 and Type-2. Both use alternating inductors and capacitors, but the starting series/shunt arrangement is different.

More LC pairs usually produce a sharper passband response, while impedance and ripple affect the calculated element values.
