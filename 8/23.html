Inductance
Diagram & Theory
Coaxial cable inductance depends on the outer diameter D, inner diameter d, cable length L, and relative permeability of the material.
Formulas
L = (μ0 × μr / 2π) × ln(D / d) × Length
μ0 = 4π × 10⁻⁷ H/m
D, d and Length are converted to meters before calculation.
Example
D = 10 mm, d = 5 mm, Length = 100 mm, μr = 1
Inductance ≈ 1.39e-8 H
Inductance ≈ 13.86 nH
