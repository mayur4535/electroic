Coaxial cable inductance depends on the outer diameter D, inner diameter d, cable length L, and relative permeability.
Formulas

Original formula preserved:

μ0 = 4π × 10⁻⁷ H/m
L = (μ0 × μr / 2π) × ln(D / d) × Length

D, d and Length are converted to meters before calculation.
Example

D = 10 mm, d = 5 mm, Length = 100 mm, μr = 1
Inductance ≈ 1.39e-8 H
Inductance ≈ 13.86 nH
