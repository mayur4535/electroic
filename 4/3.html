Diagrams & Theory

A circular wire loop inductance depends on the main loop diameter, the wire thickness, and the magnetic permeability of the surrounding material.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

L = μ0 × μr × (D / 2) × [ ln(8D / d) - 2 ]
μ0 = 4 × π × 10^-7 H/m
D = loop diameter, d = wire diameter, μr = relative permeability.
