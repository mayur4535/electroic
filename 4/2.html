Input Parameters Specification

Wire Diameter DDiameter of each conductor in the twisted pair.
Spacing SCenter-to-center spacing between the two conductors.
Dielectric εrInsulation dielectric constant around the pair.
OutputsZo, delay, inductance and capacitance per inch.

Diagrams & Theory

A twisted pair is made from two insulated conductors. Impedance depends mainly on conductor spacing, wire diameter and dielectric material.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Zo = (120 / sqrt(εr)) × ln(2S / D)
Delay = 84.72 × 10^-3 × sqrt(εr)
Inductance = 10.16 × ln(2S / D)
Capacitance = (0.7065 × εr) / ln(2S / D)
