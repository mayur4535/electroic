Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Zo = (120 / sqrt(εr)) × ln(2S / D)
Delay = 84.72 × 10^-3 × sqrt(εr)
Inductance = 10.16 × ln(2S / D)
Capacitance = (0.7065 × εr) / ln(2S / D)

About This Calculator

Estimate twisted-pair electrical behavior from physical geometry.

The CalcBoy Twisted-Pair Impedance Calculator helps electronics users estimate characteristic impedance, delay, inductance, and capacitance for two insulated conductors.

Twisted-pair wiring is used because the two conductors stay close together and share a controlled return path. This helps reduce noise pickup, magnetic loop area, and unwanted radiation compared with separated wires. But the electrical behavior is still controlled by the conductor diameter, spacing, and insulation material. A small change in spacing or dielectric constant can shift impedance enough to matter in high-speed signals, sensor cables, differential interfaces, and communication wiring.

This calculator is useful for quick design checks when building custom cables, comparing wire geometry, or estimating whether a twisted pair is close to a target impedance. It is not a full field-solver, but it gives a practical first-pass estimate for cable delay, capacitance per inch, and inductance per inch.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is twisted-pair impedance?

Twisted-pair impedance is the characteristic impedance formed by two nearby insulated conductors. It depends mainly on spacing, wire diameter, and dielectric constant.

Why does dielectric constant affect impedance?

A higher dielectric constant increases capacitance between the wires. This usually lowers characteristic impedance and increases signal delay.

What does Zo mean?

Zo means characteristic impedance. It is the impedance a signal sees when traveling along a long uniform cable or transmission line.

Does twisting pitch affect the result?

Yes. Real twisted-pair cable pitch can affect measured impedance, especially at higher frequencies. This calculator uses a simplified geometry-based estimate.

Can I use this for Ethernet cable?

It can help explain the geometry effect, but commercial Ethernet cable should be tested against cable standards because insulation, twist rate, shielding, and manufacturing tolerance matter.

What unit is delay shown in?

Delay is shown in nanoseconds per inch, which is useful for estimating timing effects over short cable lengths.

Why do spacing and diameter matter?

Spacing changes electric and magnetic field distribution between the wires, while diameter changes conductor geometry. Together they strongly affect impedance.
