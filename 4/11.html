Diagrams & Theory

Wire size selection depends on voltage drop, conductor material, current, phase, and cable run distance. Larger current or longer distance usually requires a larger AWG or MCM conductor.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Step 1: Read source voltage drop value vp, phase multiplier, material constant K, distance, and current.
Step 2: Calculate circular mil area: CM = (Phase × K × Distance × Current) / Voltage Drop.
Step 3: Compare CM with the original lookup ranges L and H.
Step 4: Return matching AWG or MCM wire size from the original size table.
