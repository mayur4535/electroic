Diagrams & Theory

Voltage drop happens because every wire has resistance. Higher current, longer cable length, smaller diameter, or higher resistivity material increases voltage drop.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Step 1: Material selection fills resistivity ρ from the original material list.
Step 2: Wire diameter is converted from AWG, inch, or mm into meters.
Step 3: If length is entered in feet, length is converted to meters by multiplying by 0.3048.
Step 4: Wire area A = π × d² / 4.
Step 5: Wire resistance R = 2 × ρ × Length / A.
Step 6: Voltage drop Vdrop = Current × R. For three phase, Vdrop is multiplied by 1.732 / 2.
Step 7: Voltage drop percentage = Vdrop / Voltage × 100.
