Diagrams & Theory

SWG is a wire gauge standard where each gauge number maps to a fixed wire diameter. The converter uses diameter lookup data and then calculates area from the round wire diameter.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Step 1: Select SWG gauge from the dropdown list.
Step 2: The calculator reads the matching diameter in inches from the original SWG lookup table.
Step 3: Diameter in mm = Diameter in inch × 25.4.
Step 4: Cross-sectional area in mm² = (π / 4) × diameter².
