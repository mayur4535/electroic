Diagrams & Theory

A real cable has capacitance along its length. With source impedance, this cable capacitance creates a low-pass filter that reduces treble above the cutoff frequency.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Step 1: Read source impedance R in ohms, cable capacitance C in pF/m, and cable length D in meters.
Step 2: Convert capacitance from pF/m to F/m by multiplying C by 10^-12.
Step 3: Total cable capacitance effect is C × 10^-12 × D.
Step 4: Fc = 1 / (2 × 3.141592654 × R × C × 10^-12 × D).
Step 5: Display cutoff frequency rounded to the nearest Hz, preserving the original calculation logic.
