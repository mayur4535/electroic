Diagrams & Theory

Solid tantalum capacitors feature standard color bands or colored dots to denote critical electrical parameters. Due to their drop-like resin "bead" shapes, printing text on their curved surface can easily lead to identification errors. Color bands allow quick optical checkups during assembly or reverse-engineering procedures.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are tantalum bead capacitors polarized?

Yes. Tantalum capacitors are highly polarized. Connecting them incorrectly can cause immediate failure or physical damage to the surrounding circuit components.

2. What standard unit is used for the base calculation?

Tantalum capacitor color codes use picofarads (pF) as their base calculation unit, which are then scaled into nanofarads (nF) or microfarads (µF) for readability.

3. How can I identify the positive terminal on a physical tantalum bead?

Normally, a small "+" symbol or a vertical stripe printed on the resin body identifies the positive terminal (anode lead wire).
