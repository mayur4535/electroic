Diagrams & Theory

Radial leaded ceramic disc capacitors consist of a single disk of high-K or low-K ceramic dielectric material coated with silver contacts on both faces, encased in protective epoxy insulation.

Because their small, curved profiles make text printing difficult, manufacturers printed colored horizontal bands. Orient the capacitor disk so the band closest to the top of the body is on your left, then read from top to bottom (towards the wire leads).

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Calculated Capacity: Capacitance (pF) = (Digit 1 + Digit 2) × Multiplier
Conversion Ratio: 1,000 pF = 1 nF | 1,000,000 pF = 1 µF
Operational Tolerances: Rmin = Value - (Value × Tolerance%) | Rmax = Value + (Value × Tolerance%)

Step-by-Step Example

Example: 5-Band Disc - Red (250V), Red (20), Black (0), Yellow (x10000), Green (±5%)
Step 1: Write down the primary voltage prefix - Red maps to 250 Volts DC.
Step 2: Read base figures - Digit 1 = 20, Digit 2 = 0. Combine them to get 20 (base value).
Step 3: Multiply by the multiplier value - 20 × 10,000 = 200,000 pF = 200 nF = 0.2 µF.
Step 4: Decode tolerance limit - Green maps to ±5% (operational scope of 190 nF to 210 nF).

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does the top color band on a 5-band ceramic disc capacitor represent?

On a standard 5-band radial ceramic disc capacitor, the top color band signifies the maximum DC working voltage rating of the component (e.g. Red represents 250V, Blue represents 630V).

2. Why are 3-band disc capacitors more common in analog circuits?

Standard low-voltage logic circuits rarely require precision tolerances or high-voltage parameters. Standard 3-Band capacitors are highly cost-effective and provide stable coupling properties.

3. How can I differentiate between Class 1 and Class 2 disc materials?

Class 1 dielectrics (e.g., C0G, NP0) are temperature-compensating and have low dielectric constant properties. Class 2 dielectrics (e.g., X7R, Y5V) use high-K materials to achieve superior capacitance densities inside small disc dimensions.

4. What standard tolerance applies if my capacitor only has 3 bands?

When there are no tolerance markings, ceramic disk capacitors defaults to standard baseline manufacturing tolerance margins of ±20%.
