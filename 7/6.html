Diagrams & Theory

When voltage is applied across an inductor, current rises linearly. The inductor reaches saturation at Isat, and the stored energy depends on inductance and current squared.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Step 1: Convert inductance from µH to H: L(H) = L(µH) / 1000000.
Step 2: Maximum ON time: Tonmax = Isat × L / V.
Step 3: Minimum frequency: Fmin = 1 / (2 × Tonmax).
Step 4: Maximum power: Pmax = V × Isat / sqrt(6).
Step 5: Saturation energy: Esat = Isat × Isat × L / 2.
Step 6: Tonmax is displayed in µs, Fmin in kHz, Pmax in W and Esat in µJ.
