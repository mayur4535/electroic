Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Step 1: Read cathode radius a, anode radius b, magnetic field B, frequency Fr and resonator count N.
Step 2: Electron mass m = 9.11 × 10^-31 kg and electron charge e = 1.6 × 10^-19 C.
Step 3: m1 = 1 - (a² / b²).
Step 4: Hull cutoff potential = ((e × b² × B²) / (8 × m)) × m1² / 1000.
Step 5: Hartree potential = ((2 × π × Fr × 10^9 × B) / N) × (b² - a²) / 1000.
