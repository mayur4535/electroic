Diagrams & Theory

This battery run time calculator estimates how long a battery lasts from capacity and device current. Real runtime can change due to battery age, temperature, battery chemistry, conversion loss, and discharge condition.

Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Battery capacity is converted to mAh.
Device current is converted to mA.
Battery life in hours = Battery Capacity in mAh / Device Power Consumption in mA
Days = Hours / 24
Weeks = Hours / 168
Months = Hours / 730
Years = Hours / 8760
Battery indicator percent = Runtime hours, capped at 100%. So 100 hours = 100% full.
