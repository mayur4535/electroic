Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Sine wave: Vrms = Vpk / sqrt(2), Vavg = 0.
Full rectified wave: Vrms = Vpk / sqrt(2), Vavg = 0.637 × Vpk.
Half rectified sine wave: Vrms = Vpk / 2, Vavg = 0.318 × Vpk.
Sine wave with DC offset: Vrms = sqrt(Vdc² + Vpk² / 2), Vavg = Vdc.
Half sine pulse: Vrms = Vpk × sqrt(F × T / 2), Vavg = 2 × F × T × Vpk / PI.
Square pulse: Vrms = Vpk × sqrt(F × T), Vavg = F × T × Vpk.
Sawtooth pulse: Vrms = Vpk × sqrt(F × T / 3), Vavg = Vpk × F × T / 2.
Trapezoidal wave: Vrms = Vpk × sqrt(F × ((B - T) + 3 × T) / 3), Vavg = Vpk × F × (B + T) / 2.
