Step-by-Step Example

L = 100 / 1,000,000 = 0.0001 H.
Energy = 2² × 0.0001 / 2 = 0.0002 J.

About This Calculator

Calculate magnetic energy stored inside an inductor.

This calculator estimates stored energy from inductance and current using the standard inductor energy formula.

An inductor stores energy in its magnetic field when current flows through it. This stored energy is important in switching power supplies, solenoids, pulse circuits, relay coils, ignition coils, boost converters and flyback transformers. Since the formula uses current squared, increasing current has a much stronger effect than increasing inductance by the same ratio.

InductanceHigher inductance stores more energy at the same current.
CurrentEnergy rises with current squared.
Magnetic FieldStored energy exists in the field around the coil.
Power DesignUseful for converter and pulse-circuit checks.
Tip: In real inductors, saturation current limits usable stored energy. Always check the inductor datasheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is inductor stored energy?

It is energy stored in the magnetic field of an inductor when current flows through it.

What formula is used?

The calculator uses Energy = I² × L / 2.

Why is inductance entered in µH?

Microhenry is common for power inductors, RF coils and converter inductors.

Why does current matter so much?

Current is squared in the formula, so doubling current gives four times the energy.

Does this include saturation?

No. This is an ideal energy calculation and does not include core saturation or losses.

What unit is the answer?

The result is shown in microjoules.
