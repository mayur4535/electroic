Formulas & Mathematical Logic

TpW = Lb / Dw
W = N / TpW
Dc = 2 × W × Dw + Db
Dcin = Dc / 25.4
Rw = (W × Dw + Db) / 2
A = π × Rw × Rw
Ain = A / (25.4 × 25.4)
L = 2 × π × Rw × N / 1000
RpM = 0.0333 × ((0.812 / 2)²) / ((Dw / 2)²)
R = RpM × L, V = I × R, P = V × I

About This Calculator

The Coil Physical Properties Calculator by CalcBoy is designed for users who need a fast way to estimate real-world coil winding dimensions and electrical behavior before building a coil. In many solenoid, relay, electromagnet, actuator, motor, speaker, sensor, and transformer experiments, the physical size of the coil is just as important as the electrical resistance. This calculator helps convert basic winding data into useful design values such as turns per winding layer, number of winding layers, final coil diameter, cross-sectional area, total wire length, resistance, operating voltage, and power consumption.

To use this CalcBoy coil winding calculator, enter the wire diameter, total number of turns, bobbin length, bobbin diameter, and DC current. The tool then estimates how many turns fit across the bobbin length, how many winding layers are required, and how large the finished coil becomes. It also estimates the average wire path radius, which is used to calculate total wire length. From wire length and resistance per meter, the calculator estimates coil resistance. Finally, using the entered current, it calculates the required voltage and the power dissipated by the coil.

This calculator is useful for solenoid coil design, electromagnet winding, copper winding estimation, relay coil testing, actuator coil planning, and low-voltage DC coil experiments. It can help compare different wire diameters before physically winding the coil. A thinner wire may allow more turns in a compact space but increases resistance and heating. A thicker wire reduces resistance but increases coil diameter and may reduce the number of turns that fit in each layer. For practical engineering work, always verify the final coil with a multimeter, temperature test, and safe current limit because real winding tension, insulation thickness, enamel coating, bobbin shape, and wire material can affect the final result.

CalcBoy provides this coil physical properties calculator as a practical design aid for students, hobbyists, repair technicians, electronics engineers, and makers who need simple coil design calculations without external software.
