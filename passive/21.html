About This Calculator

Calculate material resistivity from conductor geometry.

This Resistivity Calculator estimates electrical resistivity using resistance, length and cross-sectional area.

Electrical resistivity describes how strongly a material opposes current flow. It is different from resistance because resistance depends on the physical size of the conductor, while resistivity is a material property. A long thin wire has more resistance than a short thick wire, even if both are made from the same material. By using length and cross-sectional area, this calculator converts a measured resistance into resistivity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is resistivity?

Resistivity is a material property that tells how strongly the material resists electric current.

What is the formula for resistivity?

The formula is ρ = R × A / L, where R is resistance, A is area and L is length.

What unit is resistivity measured in?

Resistivity is commonly measured in ohm-meter, written as Ω·m.

Is resistivity the same as resistance?

No. Resistance depends on size and shape, while resistivity describes the material itself.

Why does area matter?

A larger cross-sectional area gives current more path to flow, reducing resistance for the same material.

Can I use this for copper wire?

Yes, if you know resistance, length and cross-sectional area of the copper conductor.

Does temperature affect resistivity?

Yes. Many materials change resistivity with temperature.
