Formulas & Mathematical Logic

Total LED Voltage = LED Voltage × Number of LEDs
Resistor Voltage = VIN − Total LED Voltage
Resistor = Resistor Voltage / Current
Power = Resistor Voltage × Current

Step-by-Step Example

Example: VIN = 5V, red LED voltage = 2V, LED current = 20mA, LED count = 1.
Total LED Voltage = 2 × 1 = 2V.
Resistor Voltage = 5 − 2 = 3V.
Current = 20mA / 1000 = 0.02A.
Resistor = 3 / 0.02 = 150Ω. Power = 3 × 0.02 = 0.06W.

About This Calculator

Pick the right resistor for safe LED current.

This LED Resistor Calculator helps calculate series resistor value, resistor power and recommended wattage for LED circuits.

LEDs are current-driven components. If an LED is connected directly to a voltage source without proper current limiting, it can draw too much current and fail quickly. A series resistor drops the extra voltage and limits current to a safe value. This calculator uses supply voltage, LED forward voltage, LED current and number of series LEDs to estimate the required resistor.

Resistor ValueCalculated using the voltage left after LED forward voltage drop.
PowerShows resistor power dissipation so the resistor does not overheat.
WattageSuggests a practical resistor wattage rating based on calculated power.
LED SafetyAlways stay within datasheet current and voltage limits.
Tip: Choose a resistor wattage rating higher than calculated power for safe operation, especially in enclosed circuits or warm environments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does an LED need a resistor?

An LED needs a resistor to limit current. Without current limiting, the LED may overheat and fail.

What is LED forward voltage?

Forward voltage is the voltage drop across the LED when it is conducting current.

What current should I use?

Use the LED datasheet current rating. Many small indicator LEDs are commonly used around 10mA to 20mA.

Can I connect multiple LEDs in series?

Yes, but total LED voltage must stay lower than VIN so the resistor can drop the remaining voltage.

Why does the calculator show VIN must be higher?

If total LED voltage is equal to or higher than VIN, there is no voltage left for the resistor to control current.

What wattage resistor should I choose?

Choose the suggested wattage or a higher standard value for better safety margin.

Can I use this for high-power LEDs?

For high-power LEDs, a constant-current driver is usually better than a simple resistor.

Does LED color affect resistor value?

Yes. Different LED colors have different forward voltages, which changes resistor voltage drop.
