How the reference system works

Formula result: calculated directly from the current readings. It is not replaced by the reference engine.

Reference AI: with fewer than 10 saved references, it reports that there is not enough data. At 10 or more, it compares the current Core/Clad and angle inputs with saved successful cases, selects the nearest cases, and calculates a similarity-weighted torque suggestion.

Important: This is a local reference-based calculation, not a live cloud AI model and not a certified COMKAR formula. Ten references are a minimum threshold, not proof of accuracy. Use consistent units and conditions, verify the suggestion on a sample cable, and follow the machine/cable manufacturer's limits. Never rely on angle alone to judge fiber/cable quality.
