Practical Operational Examples

Bench Testing an ATX PSU

Short the green /PS_ON wire (Pin 16 on a 24-pin block or Pin 14 on a 20-pin block) to any black COM ground pin. This turns on the power supply without a motherboard.

Serial Null Modem Cable

To establish direct PC-to-PC communication via DE9 ports, cross-connect Pin 2 (RXD) to Pin 3 (TXD) and tie Pin 5 (GND) straight through.

Custom eSATA to SATA Cable

Adaptors map eSATA differential transmit (Pins 2/3) and receive (Pins 5/6) lines directly to standard SATA internal connectors with extra shield grounds.

Pins 1 to 4 provide legacy USB 2.0 pathways, while Pins 5 to 9 carry SuperSpeed differential signals (TX and RX lanes), allowing seamless connection updates.

PC Power & Signal Transmission Theory

Computer power distribution networks and communication lines rely on low-impedance pathways and balanced signal pairs to run stable high-frequency networks. Standard ATX power supplies deliver multiple voltage rails (+12V, +5V, +3.3V, -12V, and standby +5VSB) through standardized 20-pin and 24-pin mainboard blocks. Differential signaling protocols, used in USB 3.0, DisplayPort, and Serial ATA interfaces, transmit data via inverted signal pairs to reject environmental electromagnetic interference (EMI).

Tip: Pin numbering on power supplies is usually embossed on the housing. Check closely to avoid inserting cables upside-down.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does the Power Supply On (/PS_ON) pin do on ATX connectors?

The /PS_ON pin (Pin 16 on 24-pin plugs) is a low-active signal line. When the motherboard pulls this pin to ground (zero volts), the power supply turns on its main DC voltage output rails.

2. Why is the -5V white wire missing on modern ATX power supplies?

The -5V rail was originally required for ISA expansion cards used in older IBM-compatible PCs. Intel removed -5V from the ATX specification in 2004 (v2.01) because modern motherboard buses no longer use it.

3. How does Apple Lightning handle orientation-free plugging?

The Lightning connector contains an integrated authentication chip that detects orientation. The chip dynamically maps active lane pins (L0p, L0n, L1p, L1n) to match whichever side is facing up.

4. What is the role of the CC1 and CC2 pins on USB Type-C?

The Configuration Channel (CC) pins detect plug orientation, determine cable orientation, and negotiate USB Power Delivery (PD) voltage levels between connected devices.

5. Why does eSATA not provide power like USB does?

The standard eSATA interface is designed purely for high-speed data transfer. Connecting external drives requires either an external power adapter, a dual-connection cable, or an active Power-eSATA port.

6. Why do MIDI interfaces use current loops instead of standard voltage signals?

MIDI uses optocouplers to isolate transmitting and receiving devices. Isolating connections electrically prevents ground loops, hum, and digital signal interference in studio audio networks.
